Frequently asked questions

Is DriverCareHub an official Uber support service?

No. DriverCareHub is an independent information and enquiry website. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Uber or any other transport platform.

Can DriverCareHub reactivate my driver account?

No. Only the relevant platform can review or reactivate an account. We can provide general information about preparing an appeal and finding the platform's official support channel.

How do I check my enquiry?

Use the reference number displayed after submitting your enquiry and the same email address. This single-file demonstration stores records only in the browser where they were submitted.

Where should I submit an appeal?

Use the official app or website of the platform concerned. Do not share your password or verification codes with third parties.
