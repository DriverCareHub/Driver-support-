QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Is DriverCareHub an official platform support service?

No. DriverCareHub is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Uber or another transport platform.

Can DriverCareHub reactivate an account?

No. Only the platform that owns the account can review or reactivate it. We can provide general information and help you understand how to locate official appeal channels.

Where should I submit a platform appeal?

Use the official app or website of the company concerned. Never share passwords or one-time verification codes with third parties.
